International flights (SA → Brazil): your biggest single cost — roughly R14,000–22,000 return from Johannesburg or Cape Town in December (peak). Book 2–3 months ahead.
Money-savers: fly Rio⇄SP on GOL / LATAM / Azul booked early; eat your main meal at a por-quilo (pay-by-weight) buffet at lunch; buy a rechargeable transit card in each city; most of Rio's best things — beaches, hikes, viewpoints, sunsets — are free.

🏖️ Part 1 — Rio de Janeiro (Days 1–9)

Where to stay: Copacabana or Ipanema for first-timers (safe, walkable, on the metro), or Lapa/Santa Teresa for a cheaper, artsier vibe.

Day 1 — Arrival & Copacabana

  • Land, check in, recover
  • Sunset stroll on Copacabana Beach; dinner at a boteco (casual bar)
  • Easy first night to get your bearings

Day 2 — Ipanema & Beach Day

  • Ipanema Beach — grab a spot, rent a chair, watch the scene
  • Walk to Arpoador rock for the famous sunset (free, iconic)
  • Cheap eats: por-quilo lunch, then street snacks (pão de queijo, açaí)

Day 3 — Christ the Redeemer & Santa Teresa

  • Morning: Cristo Redentor via van or the Corcovado train (book ahead)
  • Afternoon: wander Santa Teresa (hilly artist neighborhood, tram, murals)
  • Selarón Steps (Escadaria Selarón) on the way down — free and photogenic

Day 4 — Sugarloaf & Botafogo

  • Sugarloaf Mountain (Pão de Açúcar) cable car — go late for sunset over the bay
  • Budget option: hike the free trail up Morro da Urca (first hill)
  • Evening in Botafogo — lively, affordable food scene

Day 5 — Vidigal (the "video" highlight) 🎥

  • Favela Vidigal: guided experience or moto-taxi up to the top
  • Hike Dois Irmãos (Two Brothers) for arguably Rio's best panorama
  • Sunset drink at a Vidigal rooftop bar overlooking the coast
  • Go with a reputable local / community-run guide — the safe, respectful way in.

Day 6 — Lapa & Nightlife

  • Daytime: Lapa Arches (Arcos da Lapa); browse the Feira do Rio Antigo if on
  • Night: Lapa street party — samba clubs, live music, cheap caipirinhas
  • Rio's budget night-out capital

Day 7 — Tijuca Forest / Slow Day

  • Tijuca National Park — waterfalls and jungle trails inside the city (free)
  • Or a lazy beach recovery day
  • Optional: Maracanã stadium tour for football fans

Day 8 — Islands / Day Trip

  • Ferry to Ilha de Paquetá (cheap, car-free island), or
  • Beach-hop to Prainha / Grumari — wilder, quieter beaches

Day 9 — Réveillon or Rio Wrap-Up

  • If dates hit 31 Dec: Réveillon on Copacabana — wear white, 2M+ people, midnight fireworks over the sea (free to attend)
  • Otherwise: last beach morning, souvenir shopping, farewell dinner

Travel Day — Rio → São Paulo

  • Flight ~1h (SDU/GIG → CGH/GRU); cheapest booked ahead
  • Budget alt: overnight bus (~6h) saves a night's lodging

🌆 Part 2 — São Paulo (Days 10–13)

Where to stay: Vila Madalena (nightlife/art), Pinheiros (food, central), or near Paulista Ave (transit hub). SP is huge — stay near a metro line.

Day 10 — Paulista & Centro

  • Walk Avenida Paulista (car-free on Sundays — great vibe)
  • MASP art museum (free on select days / cheap)
  • Mercadão (Municipal Market) — split a giant mortadella sandwich

Day 11 — Street Art & Parks

  • Beco do Batman (Batman Alley) — free open-air graffiti gallery
  • Ibirapuera Park — SP's Central Park; free museums, great for a picnic
  • Evening: Vila Madalena bars

Day 12 — Food & Markets

  • Liberdade (Japantown) for cheap eats
  • Mercado Municipal or a feira livre street market
  • Optional: football at Allianz Parque / Morumbi

Day 13 — Flexible Day

  • Day trip: Santos (beach city, ~1h bus) or Embu das Artes (crafts town)
  • Or slow city day: coffee culture, thrift shops in Pinheiros, rooftop sunset

Day 14 — Departure

  • Last coffee and pastel de feira, then head to the airport
  • Fly home (GRU is the main international hub)

📌 Practical Notes

  • Documents: South African passport holders currently need a visa/eVisa for Brazil — check and apply early.
  • Money: carry some cash (Brazilian real); cards widely accepted. Tell your bank you're travelling.
  • Safety: in Rio, don't flash phones/jewelry on the street or beach; use rideshare at night; leave valuables at the hostel.
  • Connectivity: grab a local SIM (Vivo/Claro/TIM) or an eSIM on arrival.
  • Language: Portuguese. A few phrases (bom dia, obrigado/a, quanto custa?) go a long way.
  • Sun: December sun is intense — sunscreen, hat, water.
