# Brazil Trip — Rio de Janeiro & São Paulo (December, ~2 Weeks)

A budget-conscious 14-day summer itinerary. December is **peak season** in
Brazil (hot beach weather + New Year's / Réveillon), so this plan leans on free
and low-cost activities, hostels/guesthouses, and public transport to keep costs
down.

## Rough Cost Estimate (per person, excl. international flights)

| Category | Budget approach | Est. for 14 days (USD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lodging | Hostel dorms / cheap guesthouses (~$20–35/night) | $300–450 |
| Food | Mix of *por-quilo* (pay-by-weight) lunches, street food, self-catering | $200–300 |
| Local transport | Metro, buses, occasional rideshare | $60–100 |
| Rio ⇄ SP travel | Domestic flight (~1h) booked early, or overnight bus | $50–120 |
| Activities | Mostly free sights + a few paid (Sugarloaf, Christ, tours) | $120–200 |
| **Approx. total** | | **~$750–1,150** |

*Approximate, December-peak pricing. Réveillon week pushes Rio lodging up — book
early or spend NYE somewhere cheaper.* International airfare is separate and
usually the biggest single cost.

**Money-savers:**
- Fly Rio⇄SP on **GOL / LATAM / Azul** — book 2–3 months ahead for the cheapest fares.
- Eat your main meal at lunch at a **por-quilo** buffet (cheap, filling, everywhere).
- Buy a rechargeable transit card in each city (Rio: *Jaé/RioCard*; SP: *Bilhete Único*).
- Many of Rio's best things — beaches, hikes, viewpoints, sunsets — are **free**.

## Part 1 — Rio de Janeiro (Days 1–9)

**Stay:** Copacabana or Ipanema for first-timers (safe, walkable, on the metro),
or Lapa/Santa Teresa for a cheaper, artsier vibe.

### Day 1 — Arrival & Copacabana
- Land, check in, recover
- Sunset stroll on **Copacabana Beach**; dinner at a *boteco* (casual bar)
- Easy first night — get your bearings

### Day 2 — Ipanema & Beach Day
- **Ipanema Beach** — grab a spot, rent a chair, watch the scene
- Walk to **Arpoador rock** for the famous sunset (free, iconic)
- Cheap dinner: por-quilo lunch earlier, street snacks (*pão de queijo*, açaí)

### Day 3 — Christ the Redeemer & Santa Teresa
- Morning: **Cristo Redentor** via van or the Trem do Corcovado (book ahead)
- Afternoon: wander **Santa Teresa** (hilly artist neighborhood, tram, murals)
- **Selarón Steps (Escadaria Selarón)** on the way down — free and photogenic

### Day 4 — Sugarloaf & Botafogo
- **Sugarloaf Mountain (Pão de Açúcar)** cable car — go late afternoon for
  sunset over the bay (a splurge worth it)
- Or hike the free trail up the first hill (Morro da Urca) to save money
- Evening in **Botafogo** — lively, affordable food scene

### Day 5 — Vidigal (the "video" highlight) 🎥
- **Favela Vidigal:** guided experience or moto-taxi up to the top
- Hike **Dois Irmãos (Two Brothers)** for arguably Rio's best panorama
- Sunset drink at a Vidigal rooftop bar (e.g. the well-known spots overlooking
  the coast)
- *Go with a reputable local guide or community-run tour — it's the safe, respectful way in, and worth every real.*

### Day 6 — Lapa & Nightlife
- Daytime: **Lapa Arches (Arcos da Lapa)**, browse the **Feira do Rio Antigo**
  if it's on
- Night: **Lapa** street party — samba clubs, live music, cheap caipirinhas
- This is the budget night-out capital of Rio

### Day 7 — Tijuca Forest / Slow Day
- **Tijuca National Park** — waterfalls and jungle trails inside the city (free)
- Or a lazy beach recovery day
- Optional: **Maracanã stadium** tour if you love football

### Day 8 — Islands / Day Trip
- Boat day to **Ilha de Paquetá** (cheap ferry, car-free island) — or
- Beach-hop further out to **Prainha / Grumari** (wilder, quieter beaches)

### Day 9 — Réveillon or Rio Wrap-Up
- **If your dates hit Dec 31:** join **Réveillon on Copacabana** — wear white,
  2M+ people, midnight fireworks over the sea (one of the world's great NYE
  parties, and free to attend)
- Otherwise: last Rio beach morning, souvenir shopping, farewell dinner

## Travel Day — Rio → São Paulo

- **Flight:** ~1h, Santos Dumont (SDU) or Galeão (GIG) → Congonhas (CGH) or
  Guarulhos (GRU). Cheapest booked well ahead.
- **Budget alt:** overnight bus (~6h) saves a night's lodging.

---

## Part 2 — São Paulo (Days 10–13)

**Stay:** Vila Madalena (nightlife/art), Pinheiros (food, central), or near
Paulista Ave (transit hub). SP is huge — stay near a metro line.

### Day 10 — Paulista & Centro
- Walk **Avenida Paulista** (car-free on Sundays — great vibe)
- **MASP** art museum (free on select days / cheap)
- **Centro:** Municipal Market (**Mercadão**) — try a mortadella sandwich
  (split one, they're huge)

### Day 11 — Street Art & Neighborhoods
- **Beco do Batman (Batman Alley)** in Vila Madalena — free open-air graffiti gallery
- **Ibirapuera Park** — SP's Central Park; free museums inside, great for a picnic
- Evening: Vila Madalena bars

### Day 12 — Food & Markets
- SP is Brazil's food capital: hit **Liberdade** (Japantown) for cheap eats
- **Mercado Municipal** or a *feira livre* (street market) for fruit and snacks
- Optional: a cheap show, live music, or football at **Allianz Parque / Morumbi**

### Day 13 — Flexible Day
- Day trip option: **Santos** (beach city, ~1h by bus) or **Embu das Artes**
  (crafts town)
- Or slow city day: coffee culture, thrift shops in Pinheiros, rooftop sunset

### Day 14 — Departure
- Last coffee and pastel (*pastel de feira*), then head to the airport
- Fly home (GRU is the main international hub)

## Packing & Practical Notes

- **Documents:** check current visa rules for your nationality before booking.
- **Money:** carry a bit of cash; cards widely accepted. Notify your bank.
- **Safety:** in Rio, don't flash phones/jewelry on the street or beach; use
  rideshare at night; leave valuables at the hostel. Common-sense stuff.
- **SIM/eSIM:** grab a local data plan (Vivo/Claro/TIM) or an eSIM on arrival.
- **Language:** Portuguese. A few phrases (*bom dia, obrigado/a, quanto custa?*)
  go a long way.
- **Sun:** December sun is intense — sunscreen, hat, water.
