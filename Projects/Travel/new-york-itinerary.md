## Trip Overview

- **Destination:** Finger Lakes, New York
- **Suggested length:** 3–4 days
- **Best season:** Late spring through early fall (May–October); peak foliage in
  October
- **Getting there:**
  - **By air:** Fly into Rochester (ROC, ~50 min drive) or Syracuse (SYR, ~1h20)
  - **By car from NYC:** ~4.5–5 hours via I-80 W / I-380 N / NY-17
  - A rental car is essential — the region is rural and spread out

---

## Day 1 — Arrival & Settle In

- Arrive and check in near your saved location (Middlesex / Canandaigua Lake
  area)
- Drive the shore road along **Canandaigua Lake** for first views
- Casual dinner in **Naples, NY** (south end of the lake) or **Canandaigua**
- Sunset by the water

## Day 2 — Canandaigua Lake Loop

- Morning: **Sonnenberg Gardens & Mansion State Historic Park** (Canandaigua)
- Lunch in **Canandaigua** downtown
- Afternoon: a couple of local **wineries or cideries** along the west side
- Optional: boat tour or lakeside walk
- Evening: return toward your saved location; relaxed dinner

## Day 3 — Gorges & Waterfalls

- Day trip east to **Watkins Glen State Park** (Seneca Lake) — the gorge trail
  is the regional highlight
- Lunch in **Watkins Glen** or **Hammondsport** (Keuka Lake)
- Afternoon: scenic drive back along **Keuka Lake**
- Dinner near your base

## Day 4 — Flexible / Departure

Pick based on interests before heading out:

- **Wine lovers:** Seneca Lake or Keuka Lake wine trail
- **Outdoors:** hiking at Grimes Glen (Naples) or another state park
- **Slow morning:** breakfast in Naples, then drive out
- Depart from ROC / SYR or begin the drive back

---

## Practical Notes

- **Cell service** can be spotty in rural stretches — download offline maps
- **Cash** is handy at small farm stands and some tasting rooms
- **Reservations** recommended for popular wineries on summer weekends
- **Foliage season (Oct)** is beautiful but busy — book lodging early
